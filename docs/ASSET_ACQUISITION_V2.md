Why not a downloaded stick-figure asset for the rig test: none was needed — the skeleton is data (`rig_def.py`) and the rig test (`output/tests/rig_motion_test.mp4`) drives the same character parts.
Why the body is own-work: no CC0/permissive vector human on the market ships **separable limbs** in a consistent style; Open Peeps' and Humaaans' bodies are single illustrations. Reusing their heads and building the limbs
in the same ink language gave a rig-able character with an honest provenance (CC0 atoms + own generator).
