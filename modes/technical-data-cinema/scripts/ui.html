Use Devanagari Hindi, Roman Hindi, Hinglish, English technical terms, and normal punctuation. Optional performance cues such as [HOOK], [CURIOUS], [SUSPENSE], [EXPLAIN], and [REVEAL] are supported by your current directed-cue pipeline; use them sparingly and put each cue before the passage it should influence. [PAUSE] is converted to a natural pause, and [END] is removed before speech so neither is read aloud.
